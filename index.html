1. Introduction

Only4u ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application designed to provide convenient access to selected online services, useful links and financial calculators.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Only4u Android application.

3. Information We Collect

Only4u does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, address, phone number or password to use the main features of the application.

The calculators available in Only4u are intended to perform calculations on the user's device. The financial values entered into calculators are not intentionally collected or stored by Only4u for creating user profiles.

However, third-party services used by the application, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), may automatically collect certain information from the device for advertising, analytics, security and fraud-prevention purposes.

Depending on the applicable configuration and Google's services, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information required to provide and measure advertisements

4. Google AdMob

Only4u uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain device and usage information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies, terms and applicable laws.

5. Advertisements

Only4u may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, user choices, region and Google's advertising policies.

6. External Websites and Services

Only4u provides links to external websites and online services. When a user opens an external website, the user is interacting with that third-party website rather than Only4u.

External websites may have their own privacy policies, terms and data-collection practices. Only4u does not control the privacy practices, content or security practices of external websites.

7. Financial Calculators

Only4u may provide calculators such as:

  • EMI Calculator
  • SIP Calculator
  • Compound Interest Calculator
  • Simple Interest Calculator
  • FD Calculator
  • PPF Calculator

The calculators are provided for informational and calculation purposes only. Calculator results should not be considered financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled directly by the application. Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to the security and privacy practices of those providers.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Only4u does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally maintain a personal user account database.

Because Only4u does not maintain user accounts, there is no Only4u account that users need to delete.

Information that may be automatically processed by third-party services, such as advertising information handled by Google AdMob, is subject to the applicable policies, controls and retention practices of those services.

10. Children's Privacy

Only4u is not specifically designed as a children's application. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us.

Third-party advertising services may apply their own age-related privacy and advertising safeguards.
